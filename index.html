About DisasterShield

DisasterShield

Version 2.0 (Enhanced Edition)

DisasterShield is a state-of-the-art education system designed to prepare individuals and communities for natural and man-made disasters. Our mission is to provide accessible, life-saving information through modern technology.

Project Goals:

  • Educate the public on 10+ major disaster types.
  • Provide interactive learning tools and certification.
  • Offer real-time safety information and emergency SOS alerts.
  • Multi-language support for inclusive safety education.
